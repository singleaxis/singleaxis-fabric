This release installs only the Fabric Collector: a passive recorder that
captures, protects and delivers records without altering the monitored system.

Spec 049 P0-A (supersedes spec 048 §1): agents in node mode record content
only with a capture policy (FABRIC_CAPTURE_POLICY) and FABRIC_WIRE_ID_KEY;
without them they keep recording metadata and report why on every exchange.

Configuration acceptance is not production qualification. Identity and storage
review references are operator assertions, not runtime proof of workload
identity, encryption, capture completeness, or destination durable readback.
